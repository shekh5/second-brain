## Video description

Cognition's merged PRs grew 40x in a year while its engineering team grew 2.7x. Walden Yan explains what changed: self-driving PRs by default, small pods that own everything from bug reports to QA, and a workflow run almost entirely from Slack. He covers running every service locally so agents can test, VMs rebuilt every hour, and agents that optimize the factory itself. Then he shows the next step: giving agents jobs instead of tasks, from tuning inference on new GPUs to an Android app maintained by Devin over 44 days.
